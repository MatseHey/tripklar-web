2. Overview

tripklar. is an app for planning trips together. For accounts, shared trips, the expense splitter, packing lists, calendar and bookmarks we use Google Firebase (Authentication and the Firestore database) as a technical service provider, plus Apple for weather and place search and GetYourGuide for partner offers.

We ourselves do not use analytics or tracking software and do not embed advertising networks. The app shows labelled partner links to GetYourGuide (section 7); nothing is transmitted to GetYourGuide until you tap one of them. We do not access your contacts or your device's location.

3. Registration and account

Using tripklar. requires an account. When you register we collect:

  • Email address
  • Password (stored hashed by Firebase, never visible to us in plain text)
  • A display name of your choice

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the contract).

Your display name is visible to the members of your trips. Technically, it can only be retrieved by someone who knows your randomly assigned user ID – a list of all users cannot be retrieved.

Your email address is stored only in Firebase Authentication (sign-in, password reset), not additionally in our database.

Sign in with Apple: if you use “Sign in with Apple”, Apple gives us a user identifier, an email address (optionally an anonymous relay address from Apple) and, on your first sign-in, your name if you choose to share it. There is no password in that case. When you delete your account, we revoke tripklar.'s access to your Apple ID. Apple's privacy policy applies as well.

4. Shared trip planning

When you create a trip or join one with an invite code, we process:

  • Trip details: title, destination, dates, chosen travel styles, whether children are coming along
  • Names and user IDs of all trip members
  • Events, bookmarks, packing-list items and expenses you add

This data is visible to every member of the same trip — that is the core of the app. Private packing-list items are visible only to you. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Expense splitter: expenses record who paid how much for whom. All trip members can see this, as it is needed to settle up.

Free-text fields: please don't enter personal data about people outside your travel group without their consent.

5. Processing by Google Firebase

For accounts (Firebase Authentication) and the database (Cloud Firestore) we use Firebase by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, under a data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR.

International transfers: processing may also take place on servers of Google LLC in the United States. Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses apply in addition.

6. Other services in the app

Cover photos

The cover photos for your trips come from a fixed selection that ships with the app. No data is sent to third parties when a cover photo is displayed or chosen.

Weather (Apple WeatherKit)

For forecasts and current weather we use the weather service Apple WeatherKit provided by Apple Distribution International Limited, Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland. We send the coordinates of your destination (not your own location). No user identifier and no travel dates are sent; the app identifies itself to Apple through its own entitlement. Apple technically receives your device's IP address. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in showing the weather for your destination). To determine the destination's time zone, your device also converts these coordinates into a place name through Apple's location services.

Wherever weather data is shown, the app displays the source attribution required by Apple (“Apple Weather”) with a link to the legal notices and other data sources. Apple's privacy policy applies as well.

Place search (Apple MapKit)

Place search uses Apple MapKit with only the text you type, never your device location. Apple's privacy policy applies as well.

Calendar export (Apple EventKit)

When exporting to your iPhone calendar, iOS asks for your permission. The export is one-way and happens entirely on your device; we receive no data from it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent via the system prompt).

Purchases (Apple App Store)

You buy tripklar Plus and single trips through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we receive no payment or card data. The app checks whether a purchase exists directly on your device through Apple (StoreKit). We don't store your purchases on our servers. Apple's privacy policy applies. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

7. Partner offers from GetYourGuide (advertising)

tripklar. shows a link to offers for tours, tickets and activities from GetYourGuide. If a booking is made through these offers, we receive a commission; it does not make the booking more expensive for you. The offers are labelled in the app as “Ad: partner link to GetYourGuide” and appear only for trips that have not yet ended.

Partner links

In the trip view, under “Inspiration” and on the home screen there are buttons to GetYourGuide (e.g. “Experiences in [place]”). Only when you tap one does GetYourGuide's search for your destination open in the in-app browser (Safari). This transmits the place name, our partner ID and an identifier of the spot in the app where you tapped the link, and for technical reasons your IP address. We do not transmit your name, email address or user ID. GetYourGuide is responsible for everything that happens on its pages afterwards, including cookies and any booking. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in showing you suitable offers at your request and attributing bookings to our partner programme).

Nothing is transmitted before you tap. The button consists only of text and the GetYourGuide logo, which is built into the app. tripklar. loads nothing from GetYourGuide for it and embeds neither a script nor a web view from GetYourGuide. We do not log who taps the link, and we store nothing about it on your device or on our servers. Only GetYourGuide can set cookies or similar technologies, in its own browsing area; its own consent request applies there. You can object to our processing under Art. 21 GDPR; the easiest way is not to tap the link.

Recipient and responsibility

The recipient is GetYourGuide Deutschland GmbH, Sonnenburger Straße 73, 10437 Berlin, Germany. GetYourGuide processes the transmitted data as an independent controller for its own purposes, such as attributing bookings to the partner programme. How long GetYourGuide retains the data and which service providers it uses, including outside the EU (by its own account on the basis of standard contractual clauses), is set out in GetYourGuide's privacy policy, which also lists how to contact GetYourGuide's data protection team.

8. Share extension and local storage

Links you share into tripklar. from other apps are briefly kept in a local storage area on your device (App Group) until you save them to a trip. We also keep a few technical settings locally (e.g. the last opened trip). This data never leaves your device.

9. Retention

We keep your data as long as your account exists. A trip's content remains until the trip is deleted; deleting a trip also deletes its events, bookmarks, expenses and packing-list items.

10. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app under “Profile → Edit account → Delete account”. This:

  • deletes your sign-in account and your profile (display name),
  • removes you from trips you joined, including your name in the member list,
  • deletes trips you created on your own, with all their content,
  • hands trips you created with others over to another member, so the group can keep planning,
  • deletes your private packing-list items.

Expenses, shared packing-list items, events and bookmarks you added to shared trips remain. They are then only linked to a pseudonymous user ID and shown as “Former member”. This serves the other members' legitimate interest in a complete shared plan and settlement (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If you want these removed too, delete them in the app before deleting your account, or contact us.

12. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)), for example for the calendar export or notifications, usually in the iPhone settings.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), e.g. the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

To exercise your rights, simply email info@tripklar.app.
