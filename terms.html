2. What tripklar. offers

tripklar. is an app for private groups – such as couples, families and friends – to plan trips together. This includes in particular:

  • creating trips with a destination, dates and travel styles,
  • inviting others to a trip with an invite code,
  • a shared calendar for events and activities,
  • a shared and a private packing list,
  • a list for collecting ideas and links,
  • a budget splitter that divides shared costs,
  • weather and cover photos for the destination.

tripklar. is not a tour operator, travel agent or booking platform. No travel services such as flights, accommodation or activities are booked or arranged through the app.

The app contains a link to GetYourGuide (tours, tickets and activities at your destination), labelled “Anzeige: Partnerlink zu GetYourGuide” (“Ad: partner link to GetYourGuide”).
